Angular 22.2 is officially here, and alongside the substantial technical upgrades we analyze in this episode, the upcoming second patch of this version brings a fascinating mathematical curiosity.

In recreational mathematics, a repdigit (or monodigit) is an integer composed solely of repetitions of a single digit (such as 777 or 222). While software version strings aren't standard decimals—being dot-separated tuples of integers—the upcoming arrival of version 22.2.2 will closely mirror this property: a pattern unseen since version 11.1.1 back in 2021, and one that likely won't occur again for more than a decade until 33.3.3.
