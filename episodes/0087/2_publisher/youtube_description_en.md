Angular 22.3 is officially here, and in this episode of Angularidades we are joined by Dany Walls and Eduardo Roth, both Google Developer Experts in Angular, to discuss what this new minor release brings within the framework's release cycle.

Beyond targeted developer experience improvements, v22.3 takes concrete steps toward the future of its compilation with the Rust-based ngp, strengthens template safety, brings the library building mechanism into the CLI, and continues bringing the framework closer to modern web platform capabilities.
