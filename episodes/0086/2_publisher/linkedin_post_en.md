How do you lead and scale a digital marketplace as a non-technical founder? In this episode, we speak with Alex Villibord, founder and CEO of xhop-n-go, Six Sigma Black Belt certified in business analysis and process optimization.

From Miami, Alex shares how to translate high-level business vision into actionable engineering requirements using frameworks like MoSCoW, while tackling trust, fraud prevention, and unit economics in peer-to-peer marketplaces.

Topics we cover during the conversation:
✔️ The evolution of online marketplaces from Craigslist to xhop-n-go's commitment-based transaction model
✔️ Effective communication with engineering teams using MoSCoW prioritization and Eisenhower matrices
✔️ Mitigating financial fraud and account takeovers using AI-driven detection rules
✔️ Essential corporate and legal foundations before launching apps on Google Play and App Store
✔️ Cloud infrastructure costs, third-party APIs, and overcoming the critical 10,000-user scaling threshold
