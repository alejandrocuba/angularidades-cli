How do you turn a business vision into a scalable digital product as a non-technical founder, and how do you effectively bridge that vision with an engineering team?

Learn directly from Alex Villibord, founder and CEO of xhop-n-go, Six Sigma Black Belt certified and risk management specialist. From Miami, Alex breaks down how to build a marketplace from scratch, tackle fraud through enforced user commitments, and overcome the critical 10,000-user scaling threshold.
