In this episode of Angularidades, we talk with Raúl Jiménez about GolemUI, a library for creating and managing JSON-based dynamic forms in Angular.

Topics covered during our conversation:
✔️ The evolution of JSON forms after 10 years of development and lessons learned in large-scale projects.
✔️ Separation of concerns: Web Components (Lit) for inputs and native framework-delegated layouts.
✔️ Handling nested repeaters with unlimited depth and extreme performance optimization.
✔️ AI Integration: MCP servers, JSON schemas, and token efficiency benchmarking for LLMs.
✔️ The danger of reinventing the wheel in the AI era.
