Discover GolemUI, a new headless form engine, directly from one of its creators: Raúl Jiménez, Angular GDE. We discuss why he decided to create GolemUI, his vision for forms in the JavaScript ecosystem, and the technical challenges he and his team have been solving while taking it open source.
